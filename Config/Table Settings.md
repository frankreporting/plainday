How the tables Obsidian PM writes come out: the time report's tables, timesheets and invoices. This note is yours: updates leave it alone.

`freeze_rows` and `freeze_columns` keep a table's first rows and columns in view as you scroll it, with the [Selectable Tables](https://github.com/frankreporting/selectable-tables) plugin. Blank or 0 is off. `freeze_columns` counts from the first named column, so a timesheet's status column comes along with its date. A table you freeze or unfreeze yourself keeps your choice. The manual's *Freezing tables* says more.
