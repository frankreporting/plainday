**Flat** when the deliverable is fixed and I've done it before: brochures, logos, a one-page site. Quote from past hours and keep a `max_hours` budget on the project note so the report shows when I'm running over.

**Hourly** when the scope will move: redesigns, anything with a client team. Cap it in the contract (`contract_max`) so nobody gets surprised, and check the hours left at the top of its time report before each invoice.

**Free** time is still time. Mark my own mistakes with 🆓 so the hours stay honest without landing on an invoice.
